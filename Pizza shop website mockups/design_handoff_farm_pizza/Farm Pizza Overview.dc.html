Design brief · assumptions and reasoning

Farm Pizza.
Storefront and back office.

23 screens across two working prototypes: a customer site that orders in three clicks, and the back office that runs the shops behind it. Both are set in the Modernist system: Archivo, one red, no rounded corners, 2px rules, photography in black and white.

BrandFarm Pizza — farm-sourced ingredients, dough made daily. Three Essex shops: Grays, Chelmsford, Colchester.
MarketUK · £ · desktop-first
OrderingDelivery · Collection · Dine-in table order · Table booking
Codelaunchflowuk-sys/PizzaParty is empty, so everything here is built from scratch on the design system.
FidelityWorking prototypes: basket, builder pricing, checkout, live tracker, kitchen queue, dispatch and toggles all function.
01  Where the big three lose

Domino's, Pizza Hut and Papa John's front-load carousels, pop-ups and stacked promos. The menu is three taps deep and every price says "from". Speed is real, clarity is not.

02  How Farm Pizza wins

One order bar (mode, postcode, time) that follows you. Real prices with the size shown. A builder with a live total. A tracker that shows the oven, not a cartoon. Red is spent on exactly one action per screen.

03  The system on the page

Menu items sit in a visible modular grid, not floating cards. Sections are separated by 2px rules. Labels are flush left, even inside buttons. Photographs print in black and white; the one red field is the closing poster.
