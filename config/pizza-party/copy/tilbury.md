## Pizza delivery across Tilbury

Pizza Party is on Dock Road, in the middle of Tilbury, and has been rated 4.7 out of 5 by more than 350 customers. Dough is made fresh every day. Order online in under a minute, pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, and follow your order from the oven to your door.

We deliver across Tilbury, West Tilbury, East Tilbury, Chadwell St Mary, Grays and Little Thurrock. If your postcode starts RM18, RM17 or RM16, you are in our delivery zone.

## Why order direct

- **Four sizes**, from a 7" small to a 15" X-Large — ten slices, made to order.
- **Fresh dough daily**, with BBQ and garlic bases on request and stuffed crust if you want it.
- **Build your own.** Any four toppings on a Pizza Party Special, or two different halves on a Half & Half.
- **No app fees.** Ordering here rather than through a delivery app means the full amount stays with a local shop — and the prices are lower.
- **Not just pizza.** Grilled chicken, burgers, wraps, pasta, kids' meals and sharing boxes from the same kitchen.

## Frequently asked questions

### What time do you open?
Every evening from 3pm. We take orders until 11pm Monday to Saturday and until 10:30pm on Sunday.

### What is the minimum order for delivery in Tilbury?
£12, with a £2.50 delivery fee. Collection from 162 Dock Road has no minimum.

### How long does delivery take?
About 45 minutes. Collection is usually ready in 15.

### Can I order in advance?
Yes. If we are closed you can choose a slot for later today or tomorrow and pay now.

### Can I pay cash?
Yes, on collection.
