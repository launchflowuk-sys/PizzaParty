## Pizza delivery across Basildon

Farm Pizza on Clay Hill Road has been feeding Basildon for years. Order online in under a minute, pay with Apple Pay, Google Pay or card, and track your order live from the oven to your door.

We deliver across Basildon, Pitsea, Vange, Laindon, Fryerns, Lee Chapel, Kingswood, Langdon Hills, Dry Street, Noak Bridge, Crays Hill and Nevendon. If your postcode starts SS13, SS14, SS15 or SS16 you are in our Basildon zone.

### What is the minimum order for delivery in Basildon?
The minimum is £12 with a £2.50 delivery fee. Collection from 467 Clay Hill Road has no minimum.

### Can I order in advance?
Yes. If we are closed you can pick a time slot for later today or tomorrow and pay now.

### Do you deliver to Laindon and Pitsea?
Yes. Both are inside our SS15 and SS13 delivery zone.

### Can I pay cash?
Cash is accepted on collection. Delivery orders are paid online.
