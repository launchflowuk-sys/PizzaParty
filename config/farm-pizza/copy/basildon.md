## Pizza delivery across Basildon

Farm Pizza delivers hot, hand-stretched pizza across Basildon every evening. Order online in under a minute, pay with Apple Pay, Google Pay or card, and track your order live from the kitchen to your door.

We cover Pitsea, Laindon, Langdon Hills, Vange, Lee Chapel, Fryerns, Kingswood and Basildon town centre. If your postcode starts SS13, SS14, SS15 or SS16 you are in our Basildon delivery zone.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the minimum order for delivery in Basildon?
The minimum is £12 with a £2.50 delivery fee. Collection has no minimum.

### Can I order in advance?
Yes. If we are closed you can pick a time slot for later today or tomorrow and pay now.

### Do you deliver to Laindon and Pitsea?
Yes. Both are inside our SS15 and SS13 delivery zone.

### Can I pay cash?
Cash is accepted on collection. Delivery orders are paid online.
