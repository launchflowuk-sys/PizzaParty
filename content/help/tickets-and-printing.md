**Read this first: you are almost certainly not printing.** No receipt printer is connected to this system for this shop. The kitchen tablet screen **is** the ticket. If a printer has stopped, it is either your own hardware doing its own separate job, or nothing was ever set up here.

## You will know because

Nothing came out of the printer, or — much more likely, and much more serious — orders are not appearing on the kitchen tablet.

## Do this now

1. **Wake the tablet** and check the tab is still on the kitchen screen. If it has wandered off, sign back in.
2. **Check the top of the screen says Sound on.** If the button says **Enable sound**, tap it. Sound switches itself off after every page reload, so this is the usual culprit on a quiet-looking tablet.
3. **Reload the page.** The board asks for new orders every five seconds; a screen that has been open all day on flaky wifi can go stale.
4. **Check the wifi.**
5. **If the tablet has died**, sign in on any other tablet, laptop or phone — the shared kitchen PIN on the kitchen sign-in page, or your own PIN on the back-office sign-in page and then **Kitchen queue** in the sidebar. The board works on a phone at a push.

## What the system does and does not do

New orders appear on the {shop} kitchen screen and **nowhere else**. There is no text to the kitchen, no email to the kitchen and no printer — all three are empty in this shop's setup. **If that tab is closed or the tablet is asleep, nothing tells anybody an order has arrived.**

The one other sign is passive: in the back-office sidebar, **Kitchen queue** carries a number counting the orders live in the shop. It is useful only if somebody happens to be looking at it.

While an order is sitting in **New** and sound is on, the tablet beeps every twenty seconds until somebody accepts or rejects it.

There is no re-print and no print view, and no way to pull a ticket back into a column once you have pushed it through. What you can do is read the order: on **Orders**, tap its number and the customer's tracking page opens, showing every line, the toppings, the address and the total. **Orders** opens for a manager, a shift lead or front of house, not for a kitchen sign-in.

## If it is still wrong

Ring LaunchFlow. Switching on a kitchen alert text or a kitchen email is a setup change on their side, and it is worth asking for **before** the next busy night rather than during it.

## Afterwards

Keep the tablet plugged in with sleep switched off, and make tapping **Enable sound** part of opening up, the same as turning the ovens on.
