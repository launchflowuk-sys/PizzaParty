## The sidebar

Every back-office screen has the same rail down the left. The shop name is at the top, **Back office** under it, then the list of screens, each with a small square beside it:

Dashboard · Kitchen queue · Orders · Dispatch · Menu &amp; pricing · Deals · Promotions · Inventory · Customers · Campaigns · Marketing · Reviews · Staff · Hours &amp; pause · Delivery zones · Help.

**You only see the screens your role can open.** A kitchen hand's rail is short and a manager's is the full list. Nothing is hidden from you as a punishment — it is just the same list the system enforces, so a screen you cannot see is a screen you could not have opened anyway.

## The kitchen badge

When live orders are on, a number appears beside **Kitchen queue** in the rail. It counts every order that is not finished — waiting to be accepted, in the oven, ready, or out with a driver.

It is a workload number, not a "being cooked" number. Six on that badge can mean six orders nobody has touched yet.

## Getting to the kitchen board and back

**Kitchen queue** in the rail leaves the back office and opens the ticket board full screen, with no sidebar — it is built for a tablet on the wall, not for a laptop.

There is no link back. Use the browser's back arrow, or type the address of the screen you want. Do not press **Sign out** on the board just to get out of it — that signs the tablet out of the kitchen as well as the back office, and somebody has to sign it back in before the next ticket can be accepted.

## The bottom of the sidebar

Under the screen list you will find, in this order:

- **Your name and your role.** Worth a glance at the start of a shift. If it is not you, the last person did not sign out.
- **Open the storefront** — your own website, as a customer sees it. The quickest way to check a price change or a sold-out item really took.
- **LaunchFlow** — the agency screen. It needs LaunchFlow's own key, so it will turn you away unless you have it.
- **Sign out.**

## Help

**Help** is the last item in the rail, under Delivery zones. Everybody has it, whatever their role.

It opens a page with a search box at the top and, under it, every article you are allowed to read, in three groups: **If something has gone wrong**, **How to**, and **Reference**.

Type plain words — the ones you would say out loud, like *sold out*, *card machine*, *refund*, *stop orders*. You do not have to know what the screen is called. Where an article has a section that answers you, the result shows that section underneath and takes you straight to it.

Press **/** while you are on the help page to jump into the search box. It is ignored while you are already typing in a box, so it will not fire in the middle of a word.

If nothing matches, the page says so and points you at **What this system does not do**, which is often the real answer, and at **Who to ring**.

Two things worth knowing:

- **You have to leave your screen to read help.** It is a page of its own, not a panel over the top. Finish what you are typing first.
- **You only see your own articles.** The help list is filtered by role the same way the sidebar is, so a driver searching help never turns up an article about promo codes.

## Why your screen looks different from somebody else's

Everything in the back office comes off one list of who can open what: the sidebar, the screens themselves, the Save buttons and the help centre. It is shown in full on the Staff screen, under **Role permissions**.

That is why two people on the same computer can see two different back offices, and why a driver searching help never turns up an article about promo codes. It is not a setting that can drift out of step — there is only the one list.

## What is not in the sidebar

- **LaunchFlow.** It is below the screen list, with your name and the sign-out button, and it needs the agency key.
- **The storefront.** Also below the list. It opens your own website, not a back-office screen.
- **There is no search across orders, customers or menu items from the help box.** Help searches help. To find an order, use the search on Orders; to find a customer, use the search on Customers.
- **There are no help markers on the screens themselves.** If you want to know what a control does, open Help and search for it.
