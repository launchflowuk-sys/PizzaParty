## You will know because

The kitchen is under water, someone has not turned up, an oven has gone, or you simply need twenty minutes to catch up.

## Do this now

1. **Fastest route — the kitchen header.** Beside the shop name and its status light there is a **Pause…** dropdown: **15 min**, **30 min**, **1 hour**, **Rest of day**. It takes effect the moment you choose one, and it works with a wet thumb on the tablet. Two things to know first: customers are shown the reason **"Busy"** and you cannot change that from here, and **"Rest of day" is four hours**, not until closing — it lifts itself.
2. **If customers need to read something other than "Busy", or you need longer than four hours**, use the back office instead: **Hours & pause**, pick **Pause 15 min**, **Pause 30 min**, **Pause 1 hour** or **Pause today** from the dropdown, type into the **Reason (shown to customers)** box — *"Kitchen is flat out, back in half an hour — or ring us on {phone}"* — and press **Apply**. **Pause today** runs for a full twenty-four hours. **Hours & pause** only opens for a manager or a shift lead. If it is not in your sidebar, pause from the kitchen header and get one of them to change the wording.
3. **To start taking orders again, press Resume.** It sits in the same place you paused from: the **Resume** button on the kitchen header, or **Resume** in the dropdown on **Hours & pause** followed by **Apply**.

## What the system does and does not do

A pause stops new orders arriving straight away and shows customers a reason — yours if you paused from Hours & pause, otherwise the fixed word "Busy".

It does **not** cancel orders already in. Check the board and cook them.

Customers can still book a **pre-order slot** for later while you are paused, so a pause is not the same as being closed.

For anything longer than today, edit that day's opening times on **Hours & pause** instead of pausing — change the times, then press **Save hours**.

## If it is still wrong

Ring LaunchFlow if orders keep arriving after you paused. Give them the time you paused and the number of an order that came in afterwards.

## Afterwards

**Nothing nags you that a pause is on.** It shows in three places and nowhere else: the word **Paused** beside the light on the **Dashboard**, the **paused** pill beside the shop name on **Hours & pause**, and the status line on the kitchen header, which also gives you the time it lifts. If you edited the opening times instead of pausing, put them back — nothing does that for you either.
