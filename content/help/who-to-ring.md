## Before you ring anybody

Two minutes here saves twenty on the phone.

- **Is the shop still taking orders?** Look at the light beside **Hours & pause** on the Dashboard, or the header of the kitchen board. If it says paused, somebody paused it and forgot.
- **Is it just this device?** Open the same screen on a phone. If the phone is fine, it is the tablet or the wifi, not the system.
- **Reload the page.** The kitchen board refreshes itself every five seconds; a screen that has been sitting open all evening can go stale.
- **Try it as a customer.** Put something in a basket on your own website and get as far as the pay step. That answers most "is it us or them" questions on its own.

If a customer is unhappy, is waiting, or has been charged wrongly, **ring them back from the shop before you ring anybody else**. Almost everything on this list can wait ten minutes. A customer sitting in the dark waiting for food cannot.

## What LaunchFlow handles

LaunchFlow built and runs the software. Ring them for anything that cannot be changed from a screen:

- **The website is down**, or orders have stopped arriving altogether.
- **Card payments on the website are failing** — the card box does not appear, or customers say the payment will not go through.
- **Customers are getting no texts** — no confirmations, no codes.
- **Something you changed has come back** — a price, an opening time, a delivery band. Tell them exactly what you changed, or it will revert again on the next update.
- **A menu change**: a new product, a new size, a new topping, a photo, an allergen, a price band, a category.
- **Staff**: a starter, a leaver, a PIN that needs changing, the shop password, the kitchen PIN.
- **Stock lines and drivers**, which are set up the same way.
- **Anything the help centre says needs LaunchFlow.** If an article sent you here, that is why.

## What the shop's own people handle

- **Your own card terminal at the counter.** It is nothing to do with this system, and the website carries on taking card orders while it is down. Ring whoever supplies the terminal.
- **Refunding money already taken by card.** That is done in Stripe, by whoever in the shop holds the Stripe login. There is no refund button in this back office.
- **The tablet, the wifi and the router.** Whoever normally sorts your broadband.
- **A driver who is not answering.** That is a people problem, not a software one — follow your own procedure.
- **Your Google listing.** Replies typed on the Reviews screen never reach Google; if a review needs answering publicly, it is answered in Google itself.

## Have this ready when you ring

Whoever you ring, have these to hand. It is usually the difference between a fix tonight and a fix tomorrow.

- **The order number.** The short number on the ticket, not the long address in the browser.
- **What time it started**, and whether it is still happening now.
- **One example that failed**, and one that worked, if you have both.
- **The exact words on the screen.** Read them out rather than describing them.
- **What you had already tried** before ringing.
- **Which device and which screen** — the wall tablet on the kitchen board, or the office computer in the back office.

## Do not do these while you wait

- Do not send a campaign or turn on an automation to explain the problem. A send cannot be taken back, and it costs money per text.
- Do not press **Reload config** on the LaunchFlow screen. It puts prices, hours, roles, stock and drivers back to how they were set up, and there is no undo.
- Do not tell customers the shop is shut when the kitchen is still cooking. Pause ordering with a reason instead, so they read something true — that is on Hours &amp; pause, where you can type the words yourself.

## After it is fixed

- **Take the pause off.** Nothing reminds you it is on.
- **Put sold-out items back on.** Nothing clears them overnight.
- **Put opening hours back** if you edited a day to shut early.
- **Tell LaunchFlow about anything you want to keep**, or the next update will undo it.
- **Write down what happened** in your own book. The system keeps no record you can read back.
