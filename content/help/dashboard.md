The dashboard is the first screen most people land on. It is a glance, not a report: four numbers across the top, the shop's open light on the right, and the last ten orders underneath.

## Today's takings

The big number on the left is money taken today, with the number of orders under it.

It counts orders placed since midnight, and it leaves out three kinds:

- **Awaiting payment** — a basket that got as far as the card screen and never came back. No money was taken, so it is not counted.
- **Cancelled**
- **Rejected** — including ones you rejected on the kitchen screen and the customer was refunded for.

So this is money you have actually taken, not orders that came at you. The third tile, **Average order, 7 days**, is worked out the same way over the last seven days. The fourth, **Customers who have ordered**, is everyone on your list who has placed at least one order, ever — it is not a figure for tonight and it will only ever go up.

One thing worth knowing if the numbers ever look a pound or two short: the day starts at midnight by the clock on the server, not on your wall. {shop} trades past midnight, so a late order can land on the next day's figure.

## Live in the kitchen

This counts every order that is still open — received, accepted, being prepared, ready, and out for delivery, all added together.

It is a **workload** number, not a "being cooked" number. An order nobody has accepted yet is in it. If this number is climbing and the kitchen board looks calm, somebody is not accepting tickets.

## The open light

The little square beside the **Hours & pause** button at the top right says one of three
things:

- **Taking orders** — the shop is open and not paused.
- **Paused** — somebody has paused ordering. It stays like this until the pause runs out or somebody presses Resume.
- **Closed** — outside today's opening times.

It reads the **first shop** in your settings. {shop} has one shop, Grays, so what it says is simply the truth. It is only worth knowing about the day a second shop is added — then this light would still be Grays' light, and somebody would have to remember that.

The button next to it takes you straight to Hours & pause, which is where you change any of it.

## Recent orders

The last ten orders, newest first. Baskets that never got paid for are left out. Columns are order number, customer, delivery or collection, the shop, status and total.

**The order number is a link, and it opens the customer's own tracking page.** That page needs no login — anyone with the link can open it, and it shows what was ordered and the delivery address. It is fine to open it yourself to see where an order is up to. Do not forward that link to anybody else, and do not paste it into a group chat.

For anything older than these ten, use **Orders**.

## What this screen cannot do

Be told now rather than hunting for it:

- **No date picker.** Today is today. To look at last Saturday, use Orders and set the dates there, or export the CSV.
- **No comparison.** Nothing tells you whether tonight is up or down on last week.
- **No split.** Delivery against collection, cash against card, best-selling pizzas — none of it is here.
- **No per-shop breakdown**, for the day a second shop exists.
- **The numbers do not refresh on their own.** Reload the page to see them move.
