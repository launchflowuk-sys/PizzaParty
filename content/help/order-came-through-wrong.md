## You will know because

The ticket does not match what the customer says they wanted — wrong size, a missing item, an address that cannot be right — or the customer rings within a minute of ordering to change something.

## Do this now

1. **Ring the customer first, from the ticket.** The phone number under their name on the kitchen ticket is a tap-to-call link, so one tap on the tablet dials them.
2. **If you have not pressed Accept yet and they want it changed:** open the **Reject…** dropdown on that ticket, choose **Other**, and confirm the *"Reject #…?"* box. Then ask them to place it again on the {shop} website. If they paid by card, rejecting refunds them in full, automatically.
3. **If you have already accepted it, or it is in the oven:** there is no reject any more. Agree with the customer what you are doing — remake it, send the missing item out with the delivery, or put money back (see *A customer wants their money back*).
4. **Find it again later** on **Orders**: type their name, phone number or postcode into the **Search** box and press **Filter**. **Orders** opens for a manager, a shift lead or front of house; kitchen and driver sign-ins do not get it.

## What the system does and does not do

There is **no way to edit an order** once it has been placed — not the items, not the address, not the total. There is no "add a note to this order" anywhere in the back office.

**Reject is the only clean exit, and it only exists while the ticket is still in the New column.** Once you press **Accept**, the reject dropdown is gone and the buttons are Cooking, Ready and so on. The kitchen board has no cancel button at all.

A part-refund cannot be done from any screen here.

You can **read** an order in full, you just cannot change it. Tapping the order number on **Orders** opens the customer's own tracking page — every line, the toppings, any note they typed, the address and the total. It sits outside the back office, so use your browser's back button to come back. That link needs no login, so read it, but do not forward it to anybody but the customer.

## If it is still wrong

Ring LaunchFlow only if the ticket shows something the customer did not choose — a topping nobody picked, a size that was not on the menu. That is a fault, not a mix-up, and they will need the order number.

## Afterwards

Write down what happened in your own book. The system records none of it: a rejected order keeps the reason you picked from the dropdown and nothing else, and there is nowhere on any screen to add a note to an order.
