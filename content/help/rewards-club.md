Customers earn **one point for every pound** they spend. The points land when an order is
marked **completed**, on the food total before delivery — so an order that is cancelled or
refunded never earns any.

That half has always worked. What was missing until now was anything to spend them on, which
is why the club sat switched off: a scheme that only ever counts upwards is worse than not
having one.

## What points buy

The **What points buy** list is yours to set. Each reward has:

- **What the customer sees** — the name on their rewards page. Write it as the customer
  would say it: *£5 off your order*, not *FIXED-500*.
- **Costs** — how many points.
- **Kind** — money off, percent off, or free delivery.
- **Worth** — pounds for money off, a plain number for percent, ignored for free delivery.
- **Min order** — the basket has to reach this before the code works. Worth setting on
  anything generous.
- **Use within** — how many days the code lives once claimed.
- **Offered** — untick to take it off the list without deleting it.

## What actually happens when someone claims one

They press **Claim** on their rewards page. Three things happen at once:

1. The points come off their balance immediately.
2. They are given a **one-use code**, tied to them. Anyone else who hears it cannot spend it.
3. The code goes in at checkout like any other promotion, and the minimum order you set is
   enforced there.

**The points are spent at the moment of claiming, not at the moment of ordering.** That is
why **Use within** matters: if you set it to seven days and somebody claims on a Monday and
forgets, their points are gone. Sixty days is a sensible default. Be generous — a customer
who loses points to an expiry is a customer who tells people about it.

## Changing your mind about a reward

**Untick Offered** takes it off the list of things that can be claimed next. **Remove**
deletes it outright.

Neither one cancels a code somebody has already claimed. They spent real points on it and it
stays valid until it expires. If you have made a genuine mistake — a reward priced at 10
points that should have been 100 — take it off the list quickly, and expect to honour the
codes already out.

## Points given by hand

Points can be added or taken off a customer directly, for the apology at the counter — a
late order, a wrong topping. It needs a reason, because **the customer sees that reason** on
their rewards page next to the points. "Sorry about Friday" reads a lot better than a
number appearing from nowhere.

A balance cannot be pushed below zero.

## What the customer sees

Their rewards page shows their balance, how many more points to the next reward they cannot
yet afford, the full list of what points buy with a Claim button on anything they can
afford, any codes they have claimed and not yet used, and their points history.

The progress bar counts towards **the cheapest reward they cannot afford yet** — a real
target, not a fixed ladder. Once they can afford everything it says so instead of resetting.

## Worth knowing

- **Points are per customer, not per branch.** Earned at Grays, spendable at Basildon.
- **A claimed code is not a deal.** It is a discount code, so it stacks with the rules on the
  Promotions screen, not with the deal builder.
- **There is no expiry on the points themselves**, only on a code once it is claimed.
- **Switching the club off** hides the rewards page and stops points being awarded. It does
  not wipe anybody's balance.
