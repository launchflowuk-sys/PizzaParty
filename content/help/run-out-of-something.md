## You will know because

The last of something has gone and orders for it are still coming in.

## Do this now

1. **Go to Menu & pricing.** Type the item's name into the **Find an item** box at the top — pressing `/` jumps you straight into it — and the seven categories collapse down to what matches.
2. **Press Mark sold out** on that row. The row goes red, the button changes to **Sold out — put back on**, and the item comes off the website immediately: where the **Add** button was, customers now see a **Sold out** tag, and if it was already in somebody's basket it drops out with a note telling them so.
3. **A whole group gone — no dough, no chicken?** Do each item in turn. There is no group toggle. The count in the **Find an item** bar (*"N of 68"*) tells you how many rows your search is showing, so you can work through them.
4. **For toppings and extras**, scroll to **Options & toppings** at the bottom and click the pill. Green means on; click it and it turns red with a line through it. On every product that offers it, the topping goes pale and will not tick — it stays on the list, so a customer can see you normally do it. The group's header bar turns red and says how many are off, like *"· 3 off"*.
5. **If Menu & pricing is not in your sidebar, you are not a manager** — only a manager can open it. Nobody else can take an item off sale, so find the manager or ring them. In the meantime, if it is bad enough, pause ordering from the kitchen header and read *Stop taking orders right now*.

## What the system does and does not do

**Sold out is a switch a person presses.** Nothing counts stock down as food is sold. Selling the last one changes nothing on any screen.

**Nothing clears it overnight.** An item marked off at nine on Friday is still off at lunchtime on Saturday.

It applies to the whole menu at once, not to one shop — {shop} runs two, so marking something sold out in Grays marks it sold out in Basildon as well. There is no way to do it for one shop only.

**The Inventory screen does not do this job.** It is a shopping list you can read but not write: the only button on it is **Reorder**, which marks the line and does not message the supplier. You cannot even change the **On hand** figure — the numbers come from your setup file and never move as food sells. When a line shows **Out**, that is the screen working it out from a number nobody has updated, and it takes nothing off the website.

Two people pressing the same button at the same moment can cancel each other out. Look at the row before you walk away — it should be red and say **Sold out — put back on**.

**Sold out is not the same as Hide.** Sold out is for tonight: the item stays on the menu, clearly marked. **Hide** takes it off the website completely and is for something you have stopped doing.

## If it is still wrong

If a customer still sees the item after the row has gone red, ask them to reload the page first — their phone may be showing a page loaded earlier. If it is still there on a fresh phone, ring LaunchFlow.

## Afterwards

**Put it back on when the delivery arrives** — **Sold out — put back on** for items, and click the red pills back to green for toppings. Nothing does this for you.

**This is the single most common way a shop loses money on this system.** The small line above the heading on **Menu & pricing** counts them for you, in red: *"N sold out"*. Read it at the start of every shift.
